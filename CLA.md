# Collab Digital Twins — Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Collab Digital Twins.

This Contributor License Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) documents the rights granted by contributors to **Collab Digital Twins**, a Canadian not‑for‑profit organization (the **"Steward"**, **"we"**, **"us"**, or **"our"**), the steward of the Collab Digital Twins project and its associated repositories (collectively, the **"Project"**).

## Why this Agreement exists

The Project is released to the public under the **GNU Affero General Public License, version 3.0 (AGPL‑3.0)**. To sustain the Project and fund its long‑term public‑benefit mission, the Steward also offers the same software to organizations under a separate **Commercial License Agreement**. This is a standard *dual‑licensing* model.

By default, you retain copyright in the code you write. If your contribution reached us under AGPL‑3.0 alone, we could not include it in the commercially‑licensed edition of the software, because we would not hold sufficient rights to relicense it. This Agreement solves that: **you keep your copyright**, and you grant us a broad license that permits us to make your contribution available under *both* the AGPL‑3.0 and our commercial terms. This is the same arrangement used by other dual‑licensed open‑source projects such as MongoDB, GitLab, and Grafana, and it is what keeps the Project's open‑source and commercial editions legally consistent.

This Agreement does **not** transfer ownership of your contribution and does **not** change your right to use your own contribution for any other purpose.

## How to accept this Agreement

You accept this Agreement either by signing it electronically when prompted on your first pull request, or by submitting a signed copy to the Steward at **[legal@collabdt.org]** before your contribution is merged. The terms below apply from the moment of your first Contribution to the Project, whether or not a separate signature is recorded.

- **Individuals** — read and accept **Part A — Individual CLA**.
- **Organizations** (where your employer or another entity owns the rights in your work) — have an authorized signatory accept **Part B — Corporate CLA**, and ensure each contributing employee also accepts Part A.
